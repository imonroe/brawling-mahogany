The chain of dates that governs a live transaction — inspection objection,
appraisal, loan availability, closing — worked out from the contract rather
than typed in one at a time.

## What exists today

Task due dates, which appear on My Work, on the dashboard, and on a deal's
Tasks tab. The deal's **Dates** tab appears in the tab row but is not yet
clickable, and the dashboard's fourteen-day tile counts deals with a task due
rather than deals closing, for the same reason.

## What it will do

**Derive the chain.** Give it the accepted offer and the contract dates, and
the deadlines that follow are worked out rather than transcribed.

**Cascade.** When one date moves — and one always does — everything downstream
moves with it, instead of being corrected by hand in four places and missed in
a fifth.

**Remind.** Before a deadline, not after it, by email and by push.

It will also make the _date reached_ requirement able to clear on its own.

## Calendar

A calendar view of events and deadlines, and read-only feeds you can subscribe
to from your own calendar app, so the deadlines appear beside everything else
in your week.
