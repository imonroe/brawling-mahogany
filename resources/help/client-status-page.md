A page your client can open to see where their transaction is, without an
account and without calling you.

## How they get to it

A **magic link** — no password, no account, no app to install. A client should
not have to create credentials to find out whether their inspection is booked.

You send it from the deal's **People** tab, on the client's own row.

- **Send status page** emails them the link.
- **Copy link** puts the URL on your screen instead, so you can read it down
  the phone or paste it into a text message. Use this the moment somebody says
  the email never arrived.
- **Revoke access** stops it working, straight away.

The row also tells you whether they have opened it, and when they last did.
That is often the most useful thing on the screen: a client who has opened it
six times has a question they have not asked you.

### How long a link lasts

The emailed link works **once, for thirty minutes**. Opening it puts them on
the page and keeps them signed in there for a fortnight, so a client who comes
back next week does not need a new link. After that, or if you revoke it, they
see a page explaining what happened with a box to ask for a new one — which
needs nothing but their email address.

Sending a new link replaces the old one. There is never a second working URL
you have forgotten about.

## How it speaks to them

Three rules, and they come from what practitioners said rather than from a
style guide.

**No jargon.** The client sees the milestone label, never the internal stage
name. _"Your home is on the market"_, not _"Property Listed"_. This is why
milestone labels exist as their own field — and a stage you have not labelled
is simply left off their page rather than shown under its internal name.

**No instructions.** The page states facts; it never assigns the client
homework. _"Your inspection is scheduled for Thursday"_ is right. _"Action
needed: confirm inspection time"_ is not — chasing a client through a checklist
reads as less professional than a phone call, not more.

**No alarming words.** Blocked, failed, overdue and error never reach a client.
A stage you are stuck on shows as _Happening now_. A stage you skipped is not
shown at all. A deadline that has passed is not listed.

## What they see

- Where the transaction is, in one sentence, and that there is nothing they
  need to do. That second line is on the page in every state, including the
  quiet weeks — which is when people check.
- The steps: what is finished, what is happening now, what is coming.
- Important dates that are still ahead.
- Documents you marked **client-visible**, and only those.
- Your name and number.

Never your internal notes, your requirements, your tasks, or anything you did
not mark for them.

## Making a document visible

Documents are internal by default. On the deal's Documents tab, change a
document's visibility to **client-visible** and it appears on their page as a
download. Nothing else about it does — not the category, not whether we could
read it, not who uploaded it.

## Your branding

The page carries your team's name, your logo, and your accent colour from
**Settings → Team**. If your accent is too pale for white text, we work out
whether black or white reads better on it rather than leaving your client
squinting — you keep the colour you chose.
