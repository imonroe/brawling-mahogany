A page your client can open to see where their transaction is, without an
account and without calling you.

## What exists today

The **client-visible** toggle on notes, and the milestone labels on template
stages. Both are the raw material; the page that renders them is still to come.

## How it will get to them

A **magic link** — no password, no account. A client should not have to
create credentials to find out whether their inspection is booked.

## How it will speak to them

Three rules, and they come from what practitioners said rather than from a
style guide.

**No jargon.** The client sees the milestone label, never the internal stage
name. _"Your home is on the market"_, not _"Property Listed"_. This is why
milestone labels exist as their own field.

**No instructions.** The page states facts; it never assigns the client
homework. _"Your inspection is scheduled for Thursday"_ is right. _"Action
needed: confirm inspection time"_ is not — chasing a client through a
checklist reads as less professional than a phone call, not more.

**No alarming words.** Blocked, failed, overdue and error never reach a
client. If something is late, you handle it by phone.

## What they will see

Where the transaction is, what has happened, what is coming, the documents you
marked visible, and how to reach you. Never your internal notes, your
requirements, or anything you did not mark for them.
