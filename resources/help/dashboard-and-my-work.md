## My Work

Every task assigned to you, across every deal, most urgent first.

Grouped by deal, so you can see that three of today's items are all the same
transaction. The groups fall out of the ordering rather than fighting it: the
deal holding the most overdue thing sits at the top.

The count beside **My Work** in the sidebar is on every screen, so you always
know whether anything is waiting without going to look.

## The dashboard

The same question asked about the whole team.

**Four numbers** across the top, then **Needs attention** — the deals that are
blocked or late — above the full list. That split is the point of the screen:
scanning twenty-five rows for the three that matter is exactly the work it
should be doing for you.

Down the right: what is due soon, and what the team has been doing lately.

## Two of the numbers say what they mean, carefully

**Blocked stages** is captioned _"as of the last advance"_, and the caption is
honest rather than decorative. The app works out what is blocking a deal when
somebody tries to advance it. If a colleague satisfied a requirement this
morning and nobody has pressed Advance since, this number has not caught up.

Re-checking every requirement on every deal on every page load would cost more
than the number is worth. Open a deal and you get the live answer.

**Due in 14 days** counts tasks, not closings. Key dates arrive in a later
release, and until they do a heading claiming a closing date would be claiming
something the app does not know.
