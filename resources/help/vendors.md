Vendors are the people you engage rather than work for: inspectors, stagers,
photographers, lenders, title officers, contractors.

They live in the same directory as everybody else, on their own segment.

## Specialties

A vendor carries **specialties** — free text, several at a time. A stager who
also does photography is both, and gets found under either.

Free text rather than a fixed list, deliberately. The trades you engage are
yours, and a dropdown somebody else wrote will always be missing the one you
need on the day you need it. The filter offers the specialties actually in
use, so the list stays honest without anybody maintaining it.

## Finding one

Two filters: **specialty** and **service area**. Between them they answer the
question you actually have — _"who stages in Boulder"_ — in one step.

## Last used

Each vendor shows when you last had them on a deal. It is worked out from the
deals themselves rather than stored, so it is right without anybody
maintaining it.

This is the most useful column for the thing vendors are for: not _"who do I
know"_ but _"who have I actually used, and recently"_.

## Somebody can be both

A stager can be a past client and a vendor at the same time. The directory
handles that — being a vendor is a flag, not a category that displaces
anything else.
