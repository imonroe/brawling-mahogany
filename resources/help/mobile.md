## What exists today

The app works on a phone. Every screen is responsive, and the tap targets are
sized for a thumb rather than a mouse.

Notifications work too — the bell, and emails if you want them. See
[Notifications](/help/notifications).

## What is coming

**Push**, so a deadline reaches you when you are not looking at the app.

**Installing it** to your home screen, so it opens like an app, and a sensible
offline state instead of an error page when you are between signals.

On an iPhone those two are the same job: push only works once the app has been
added to the home screen, so the installing part comes first whether or not it
is the part you wanted.

## What will not change

The phone will not become a cut-down version. The two screens that matter on a
phone — My Work and the deal you are standing outside — are the two the app
was laid out around.
