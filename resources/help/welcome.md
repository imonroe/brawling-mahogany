This is the part of your business that is not selling: the sequence of things
that has to happen between an accepted offer and a set of keys, and the
question of which of them has not happened yet.

It keeps three things in one place — **your deals**, **the people on them**,
and **the process you follow** — and it is opinionated about one idea. A deal
does not move forward because somebody thinks it has. It moves forward when
the things that were supposed to happen have happened, and the app knows what
those things were.

## The shape of a week

Two screens carry most days.

**My Work** is every task assigned to you, across every deal, with the most
urgent at the top. It is the screen to open first thing.

**The dashboard** is the same question asked about the whole team: what is
moving, what is stuck, and what is due. It is the screen to open when you are
deciding where to put an afternoon.

Everything else is reached when you need it — a deal when you are working on
it, a person when you are looking them up.

## The words this app uses

A few terms mean something specific here, and the app is consistent about
them. Learning the five is worth the two minutes.

| Word            | What it means                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Deal**        | One transaction — a sale, a purchase, or a rental placement                                                            |
| **Stage**       | A period within a deal, like _Under Contract_. It has a start, an end, and work inside it                              |
| **Task**        | A single thing somebody has to do                                                                                      |
| **Requirement** | A condition that must be true before a stage can finish. Called a **gate** where you build one, in the template editor |
| **Advance**     | Moving a deal from one stage to the next                                                                               |

You will also meet **Template** — a process written down once so it can be
reused — and **Workflow**, which is what you get when a template is applied to
a real deal.

## What it does not do

Worth saying plainly, so you are not looking for it.

It is **not where contracts get signed**. Your e-signature platform stays
where it is; this app links to what lives there rather than replacing it.

It is **not a listing feed**. It links out to the MLS and to listing sites
rather than copying their content, which is a licensing matter rather than a
missing feature.

It is **not your accounting**. Offers record what was agreed so that dates and
deadlines can follow from them, not so that commissions can be calculated.
