The bell in the top bar collects everything the app wants to tell you: a task
assigned to you, a deadline coming up, something on a deal that needs a look.

A blue dot means there is something unread. Opening the bell shows the most
recent; **See all notifications** shows the rest.

## Things that arrive together arrive as one line

Attaching a workflow to a deal can hand you a dozen tasks at once. That reads
as _"12 tasks were assigned to you"_ on one line, not twelve lines — the count
tells you how many, and opening the deal shows you which.

One kind of notification is deliberately never folded: an **override**. If
somebody advanced a stage over a requirement that was not met, that is worth
reading one at a time, because each one is recorded permanently and each one
has its own reason attached.

## If you are in more than one team

You see everything, from every team you are in, whichever team you are
currently working in. Switching teams does not hide anything — a task assigned
to you at nine is still there at ten.

When more than one team has something for you, each line says which. Opening a
line from another team switches you into that team on the way, so one click
works from wherever you happen to be.

**Leaving a team takes its notifications with it.** Once your access to a team
is removed, that team's lines go from your bell — including any you had not
read. They are not yours to see any more, and the deal names on them would
otherwise keep updating.

## What you hear about, and how

**Settings → Notifications.** Each kind of thing has its own row, and you
choose whether it also reaches you by email.

**The app always keeps the notification.** That is not a setting, and it is
deliberate: turning off the email should mean "do not email me", not "do not
tell me". If you want fewer emails, switch them off and the bell still has
everything.

Out of the box you get an email for the two that are about **you** and
time-sensitive — a task assigned to you, and a deadline tomorrow. The rest
start as the bell only. Nothing has to be switched off to stop being spammed.

## Quiet hours

Set a **From** and **Until** time and no email will be sent to you between
them. It is one window, not one per kind of thing.

**Nothing is dropped.** Anything that would have gone out during your quiet
hours is sent when the window ends — and the notification itself is in the bell
straight away, so if you open the app at half past six it is already there.
Quiet hours are about not being woken, not about not being told.

The times are your **team's** timezone, shown on the settings screen. If you
work in two teams in different places, each has its own window.

## What is still to come

**Push notifications**, so a deadline reaches you without opening the app.
That needs the app installed to your home screen first, and both arrive
together in a later release.
