## Notes

Notes are free text on a deal, for the things that do not fit anywhere else.

Every note is **internal by default**. There is an explicit toggle to make one
visible to the client, and it is off unless you turn it on, every single time.
Making one note client-visible last Tuesday does not quietly publish the next
one.

The confirmation tells you which of the two just happened, because they are
different acts with different audiences and they look alike on a list.

## Activity

The **Activity** screen is the record of what the team has been doing —
stages advanced, tasks completed, contacts logged, people added. Filter it by
category using the tabs.

Activity is not the same as the **audit log**. Activity is the working record
of the business; the audit log is the security record — who signed in, who
changed a permission, who overrode a requirement — and it lives in Settings
behind its own permission, cannot be edited by anybody, and is kept to a
different standard.

## Logging a contact

Recording a call, an email or a meeting takes two clicks: pick the type, press
Log it. Everything else is optional — leaving the time blank means now, and
leaving the note blank means there was nothing worth typing.

There are three ways in. From a person's page, from a participant row on a
deal, and from the **Log contact** button in the top bar. The first two
already know who you spoke to; only the top-bar one has to ask, because the
call you need to log usually happens while you are looking at something else
entirely.

Logging from a deal's participant row attaches the deal as well, so the entry
appears on the person, the deal, and the team feed without being written three
times.
