What happens after a deal closes: staying in touch with past clients in a way
that is worth their time.

## What exists today

The **Past Client** lifecycle state, and the contact log. You can record that
you called somebody; the app does not yet remind you to.

## What it will do

**Closing a deal** will move the client into a follow-up sequence rather than
leaving them on a list.

**Prompts rather than sends.** A nudge that this is somebody's one-year
anniversary, or that you have not spoken in six months — surfaced to you, for
you to act on. The distinction matters: an automated card is a card from a
computer, and everybody can tell.

**A suggestions queue** of who is worth contacting this week and why.

## What it will not do

It will not become an automated sequence that mails your past clients on a
schedule and calls it a relationship. The name of this section is the whole
argument: keeping in touch is something you do, and the app's job is to
remind you when it is worth doing.

Anything that does send will carry an unsubscribe link and a physical address,
because that is the law for commercial email and not a preference.
