4.3. Earnest Money. The Earnest Money will be delivered to Longs Peak Title
Company, Pearl Street office, no later than the Alternative Earnest Money
Deadline.

5. FINANCING CONDITIONS AND OBLIGATIONS. Buyer will apply for a conventional
loan on or before the New Loan Application Deadline. If the terms of the New
Loan are not satisfactory to Buyer in Buyer's sole subjective discretion,
Buyer may terminate by written notice received on or before the New Loan Terms
Deadline. If the New Loan is not available to Buyer on or before the New Loan
Availability Deadline, this Contract will terminate.

6. APPRAISAL PROVISIONS. If the Property does not appraise for at least the
Purchase Price, Buyer may object on or before the Appraisal Deadline. Absent
written agreement by the Appraisal Resolution Deadline, this Contract
terminates and the Earnest Money is returned to Buyer.

7. DUE DILIGENCE DOCUMENTS. Seller will deliver the association declarations,
bylaws, current budget, minutes of the two most recent meetings, and the
reserve study on or before the Due Diligence Documents Delivery Deadline.

8. TITLE. Seller will deliver the title commitment by the Record Title
Deadline and copies of any unrecorded documents affecting the Property by the
Off-Record Title Deadline. Buyer may object in writing by the Record Title
Objection Deadline, and any unresolved objection terminates this Contract at
the Title Resolution Deadline.

9. NEW ILC OR NEW SURVEY. Buyer will order a New Improvement Location
Certificate at Buyer's expense to be received on or before the New ILC or New
Survey Deadline, and may object to any matter shown on it on or before the New
ILC or New Survey Objection Deadline.

10. INSPECTION AND DUE DILIGENCE. Buyer may have the Property inspected at
Buyer's expense and may deliver a written Inspection Objection on or before
the Inspection Objection Deadline, or terminate without cause on or before the
Inspection Termination Deadline. An unresolved objection terminates this
Contract at the Inspection Resolution Deadline. Buyer may terminate on account
of the cost or availability of property insurance on or before the Property
Insurance Objection Deadline.

12. CLOSING. Closing will take place on the Closing Date at the offices of
Longs Peak Title Company, 1832 Pearl Street, Boulder, Colorado.

17. POSSESSION. Possession will be delivered on the Possession Date at the
Possession Time.

28. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS.

28.1. Buyer will be named as an additional insured on the association master
policy at Closing, and Seller will request written confirmation from the
association manager.

28.2. Seller will provide a copy of the most recent radon mitigation system
service record within five days of MEC.
