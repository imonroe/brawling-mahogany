2. TERM.

2.1. The term of this Lease begins on the Lease Commencement Date and
continues for twelve months, ending on the Lease Expiration Date, after which
it continues month to month unless either party gives thirty days written
notice.

4. RENT AND DEPOSIT.

4.1. Rent. $2,350.00 per month, due on the first day of each month, payable to
the Placing Brokerage for the first month and to Landlord thereafter.

4.2. Security Deposit. $2,350.00, to be delivered on or before the Security
Deposit Deadline. The deposit will be held in accordance with the Colorado
security deposit statute and returned, less lawful deductions, within thirty
days of the end of the tenancy.

4.3. Prorated Rent. None. The term begins on the first day of a month.

5. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, internet and trash.
Landlord is responsible for water and sewer and for the association dues.

6. MOVE-IN INSPECTION. Tenant will complete and return the move-in inspection
form on or before the Move-In Inspection Deadline. Any condition not noted on
the returned form is presumed to have been in good order at the beginning of
the tenancy.

7. MAINTENANCE. Landlord will maintain the heating and cooling equipment, the
plumbing and the appliances supplied with the unit. Tenant will replace
furnace filters quarterly and will report any water intrusion within
twenty-four hours.

8. OCCUPANCY. The Premises may be occupied only by the persons named above and
by any child born to or adopted by them during the term.

9. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS.

9.1. One cat under fifteen pounds is permitted, with a non-refundable pet fee
of four hundred dollars payable with the security deposit.

9.2. Landlord will service the swamp cooler before the Lease Commencement Date
and annually thereafter, and will provide Tenant with the service record on
request.

9.3. Smoking is prohibited anywhere on the Premises, including the balcony and
the assigned parking space.
