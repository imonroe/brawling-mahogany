4. PURCHASE PRICE AND TERMS.

4.1. Price and Terms.

    Purchase Price ................................. $742,500.00
    Earnest Money .................................. $ 15,000.00

    Cash at Closing ................................ $133,500.00

The Purchase Price is Seven Hundred Forty-Two Thousand Five Hundred and 00/100
Dollars ($742,500.00). The New Loan is eighty percent of the Purchase Price.
The Earnest Money is Fifteen Thousand and 00/100 Dollars ($15,000.00).

4.2. Seller Concessions. Seller will credit Buyer an amount not to exceed
$7,425.00 toward Buyer's loan costs and prepaid items.

4.3. Earnest Money. The Earnest Money of $15,000.00 will be delivered to
Belmar Title Group in good funds on or before the Alternative Earnest Money
Deadline. If this Contract terminates under Sections 5, 6, 8 or 10, the entire
$15,000.00 will be returned to Buyer.

5. FINANCING CONDITIONS AND OBLIGATIONS. Buyer will apply for a conventional
loan of $594,000.00 on or before the New Loan Application Deadline. Buyer may
terminate on account of the terms of the loan by written notice received on or
before the New Loan Terms Deadline. If the loan is not available to Buyer by
the New Loan Availability Deadline, this Contract terminates.

6. APPRAISAL PROVISIONS. If the appraised value of the Property is less than
$742,500.00, Buyer may deliver a written objection on or before the Appraisal
Deadline. If the parties have not agreed in writing by the Appraisal
Resolution Deadline, this Contract terminates and the Earnest Money is
returned.

8. TITLE. Seller will deliver the title commitment by the Record Title
Deadline, Buyer may object by the Record Title Objection Deadline, and an
unresolved objection terminates this Contract at the Title Resolution
Deadline.

10. INSPECTION AND DUE DILIGENCE. Buyer may inspect at Buyer's expense, object
in writing by the Inspection Objection Deadline, or terminate without cause by
the Inspection Termination Deadline. An unresolved objection terminates this
Contract at the Inspection Resolution Deadline. Buyer may terminate on account
of the cost or availability of property insurance by the Property Insurance
Objection Deadline.

12. CLOSING. Closing will occur on the Closing Date at Belmar Title Group, 275
South Teller Street, Lakewood, Colorado. Closing costs will be allocated as
customary in Jefferson County, with the documentary fee of $74.25 paid by
Buyer.

17. POSSESSION. Possession will be delivered on the Possession Date at the
Possession Time.

28. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS.

28.1. Seller will pay for a one-year home warranty for Buyer at a cost not to
exceed six hundred fifty dollars ($650.00).

28.2. Buyer will receive a credit at Closing equal to one half of the January
through March utility charges for the Property, as evidenced by the statements
Seller will deliver to Buyer before the Record Title Deadline.
