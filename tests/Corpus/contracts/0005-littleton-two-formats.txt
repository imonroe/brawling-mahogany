5. FINANCING CONDITIONS AND OBLIGATIONS. Buyer will apply for a conventional

6. APPRAISAL PROVISIONS. Buyer may object in writing if the Property does not
appraise for at least the Purchase Price. The Appraisal Deadline is February
15, 2026 and the Appraisal Resolution Deadline is February 20, 2026. If the
objection is not resolved in writing by the Appraisal Resolution Deadline,
this Contract terminates.

8. TITLE. Seller will deliver the title commitment on or before January 29,
2026. Buyer may deliver a written title objection on or before February 3,
2026. Any objection not resolved in writing by February 8, 2026 terminates
this Contract and the Earnest Money is returned to Buyer.

10. INSPECTION AND DUE DILIGENCE. Buyer may have the Property inspected at
Buyer's expense. Buyer may deliver a written Inspection Objection on or before
January 31, 2026, or may terminate this Contract without stating a reason on
or before February 2, 2026. An objection not resolved in writing by February
5, 2026 terminates this Contract. Buyer may terminate on account of the cost
or availability of property insurance on or before February 7, 2026.

12. CLOSING. Closing will occur on March 5, 2026 at the offices of Columbine
Land Title Company, 6900 South Broadway, Littleton, Colorado.

17. POSSESSION. Possession will be delivered to Buyer on March 5, 2026 upon
