5. FINANCING CONDITIONS AND OBLIGATIONS. Buyer will apply for a VA guaranteed
loan on or before the New Loan Application Deadline. If the terms of the loan
are not satisfactory to Buyer in Buyer's sole subjective discretion, Buyer may
terminate by written notice received on or before the New Loan Terms Deadline.

this Contract terminates.

6. APPRAISAL PROVISIONS. If the Notice of Value issued by the Department of
Veterans Affairs states a reasonable value below the Purchase Price, Buyer may
deliver a written objection on or before the Notice of Value Deadline shown at
Item 8. If the parties have not agreed in writing by the Appraisal Resolution
Deadline, this Contract terminates and the Earnest Money is returned.

8. TITLE. Seller will deliver the title commitment by the Record Title
Deadline. Buyer may object by the Record Title Objection Deadline. An
unresolved objection terminates this Contract at the Title Resolution
Deadline.

10. INSPECTION AND DUE DILIGENCE. Buyer may have the Property inspected at
Buyer's expense, may object in writing on or before the Inspection Objection
Deadline, and may terminate without cause on or before the Inspection
Termination Deadline. An objection not resolved in writing by the Inspection
Resolution Deadline terminates this Contract. Buyer may terminate on account
of property insurance on or before the Property Insurance Objection Deadline.

12. CLOSING. Closing will occur on the Closing Date at Pikes Peak Closing
Services LLC, 1120 North Circle Drive, Colorado Springs, Colorado.

17. POSSESSION. Possession will be delivered on the Possession Date at the
Possession Time.

28. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS.

28.1. Seller will pay the VA non-allowable closing costs customarily charged
to a veteran buyer, including the tax service fee and the lender's document
preparation fee.

28.2. This Contract is contingent on Buyer obtaining a Certificate of
Eligibility acceptable to Lender on or before the New Loan Terms Deadline.

29. VA AMENDATORY CLAUSE. It is expressly agreed that, notwithstanding any
other provision of this Contract, Buyer will not incur any penalty by
forfeiture of earnest money or otherwise be obligated to complete the purchase
of the Property if the Purchase Price exceeds the reasonable value of the
Property established by the Department of Veterans Affairs.
