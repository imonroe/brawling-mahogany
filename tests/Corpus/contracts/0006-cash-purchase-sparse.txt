8. TITLE. Seller will deliver the title commitment on or before the Record
Title Deadline. Buyer may deliver a written objection on or before the Record
Title Objection Deadline. Any objection not resolved in writing by the Title
Resolution Deadline terminates this Contract and the Earnest Money is returned
to Buyer.

10. INSPECTION AND DUE DILIGENCE. Buyer may have the Property inspected at
Buyer's expense. Buyer may deliver a written Inspection Objection on or before
the Inspection Objection Deadline, or may terminate this Contract without
stating a reason on or before the Inspection Termination Deadline. An
objection not resolved in writing by the Inspection Resolution Deadline
terminates this Contract. Buyer may terminate on account of the cost or
availability of property insurance on or before the Property Insurance
Objection Deadline.

12. CLOSING. Closing will occur on the Closing Date at Coal Creek Title
Company, 1015 Main Street, Louisville, Colorado. Because there is no lender,
the parties agree that Closing may be moved earlier by written agreement.

17. POSSESSION. Possession will be delivered on the Possession Date upon
delivery of the deed.

28. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS.

28.1. Buyer will provide proof of funds from a Colorado depository within

stated in Section 4.

28.2. The Property is sold in its present condition. Seller will make no
repairs and will offer no credit in lieu of repairs. Buyer retains the right
