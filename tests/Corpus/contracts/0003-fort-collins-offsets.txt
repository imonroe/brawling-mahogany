4. PURCHASE PRICE AND TERMS.

    Purchase Price ................................. $612,000.00
    Earnest Money .................................. $ 10,000.00
    New Loan (Section 5) ........................... $489,600.00
    Cash at Closing ................................ $112,400.00

4.3. Earnest Money. The Earnest Money will be delivered to Cache la Poudre
Title and Trust in good funds on or before the Alternative Earnest Money
Deadline stated above.

5. FINANCING CONDITIONS AND OBLIGATIONS. Buyer will apply for a conventional
loan of $489,600.00 on or before the New Loan Application Deadline. If the
interest rate, loan costs or other terms are not satisfactory to Buyer in
Buyer's sole subjective discretion, Buyer may terminate this Contract by
written notice received by Seller on or before the New Loan Terms Deadline,
being the twenty-seventh day after MEC.

6. APPRAISAL PROVISIONS. Buyer may object in writing if the Property does not
appraise for the Purchase Price, on or before the Appraisal Deadline. If the
objection is not resolved in writing by the Appraisal Resolution Deadline,
this Contract terminates.

8. TITLE. Seller will deliver the title commitment on or before the Record
Title Deadline. Buyer may object in writing on or before the Record Title
Objection Deadline. Any objection not resolved in writing by the Title
Resolution Deadline terminates this Contract and the Earnest Money is returned
to Buyer.

10. INSPECTION AND DUE DILIGENCE. Buyer may have the Property inspected at
Buyer's expense. Buyer may deliver a written Inspection Objection on or before
the Inspection Objection Deadline, or terminate this Contract without stating
a reason on or before the Inspection Termination Deadline. An objection not
resolved in writing by the Inspection Resolution Deadline terminates this
Contract. Buyer may terminate on account of the cost or availability of
property insurance on or before the Property Insurance Objection Deadline.

12. CLOSING. Closing will occur on the Closing Date at the offices of Cache la
Poudre Title and Trust, 400 East Mountain Avenue, Fort Collins, Colorado.

17. POSSESSION. Possession will be delivered to Buyer on the Possession Date
upon delivery of the deed.

28. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS.

28.1. Seller will leave all window coverings, including the plantation
shutters in the front room.

28.2. Buyer will assume the existing solar lease with Front Range Solar
Partners LLC, subject to the approval of Buyer's lender and to Buyer
qualifying under the lease transfer terms. If Buyer's lender declines to
permit the assumption, Buyer may terminate this Contract on or before the New
Loan Terms Deadline.
