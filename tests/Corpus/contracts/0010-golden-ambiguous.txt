4.3. Earnest Money. The Earnest Money is due within three days after MEC
unless an Alternative Earnest Money Deadline is stated at Item 1 above, in
which case the Alternative Earnest Money Deadline governs and the earlier date
does not apply.

5. FINANCING CONDITIONS AND OBLIGATIONS. Buyer will apply for a conventional
loan on or before the New Loan Application Deadline. If the interest rate,
loan costs or other terms of the loan are not satisfactory to Buyer in Buyer's
sole subjective discretion, Buyer may terminate this Contract by written
notice received by Seller on or before April 3, 2026, being the New Loan Terms
Deadline stated at Item 6.

6. APPRAISAL PROVISIONS. Buyer may object in writing if the Property does not
appraise for at least the Purchase Price, on or before the Appraisal Deadline
stated at Item 8, which is the same day as the New Loan Terms Deadline. If the
objection is not resolved in writing by the Appraisal Resolution Deadline,
this Contract terminates.

8. TITLE. Seller will deliver the title commitment on or before the Record
Title Deadline. Buyer may deliver a written objection on or before the Record
Title Objection Deadline stated at Item 3. Any objection not resolved in
writing by the Title Resolution Deadline terminates this Contract.

10. INSPECTION AND DUE DILIGENCE.

10.3. Buyer may have the Property inspected at Buyer's expense, may object in
writing on or before the Inspection Objection Deadline, and may terminate
without stating a reason on or before the Inspection Termination Deadline. An
objection not resolved in writing by the Inspection Resolution Deadline
terminates this Contract.

10.5. Property Insurance. If the cost or availability of property insurance is
not satisfactory to Buyer, Buyer may terminate this Contract by written notice
received by Seller on or before the twentieth (20th) day after MEC.

12. CLOSING. Closing will occur on or before the forty-fifth day following
MEC, at the offices of Clear Creek Abstract and Title, 1201 Washington Avenue,
Golden, Colorado. The parties have entered that day at Item 14 above and
neither party may rely on a different reading of this Section.

17. POSSESSION. Possession will be delivered to Buyer on the Possession Date
upon delivery of the deed.

28. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS.

28.1. Seller will remove the shed on the east side of the lot before
Possession, at Seller's expense, and will repair any damage to the lawn caused
by its removal.

28.2. Buyer waives the right to object to the location of the fence as
disclosed on the improvement location certificate prepared for the Property
and dated August 14, 2024.
