4. PURCHASE PRICE AND TERMS.

4.1. Price and Terms.

    Purchase Price ................................. $685,000.00
    Earnest Money .................................. $ 12,000.00

    Cash at Closing ................................ $125,000.00

4.2. Seller Concessions. At Closing, Seller will credit Buyer $5,000.00 toward
Buyer's closing costs, loan discount points, prepaid items and any other fee,
cost or expense permitted by Buyer's lender.

4.3. Earnest Money. The Earnest Money of $12,000.00 will be delivered to Front

5. FINANCING CONDITIONS AND OBLIGATIONS.

5.1. New Loan Application. Buyer will make an application for the New Loan on
or before the New Loan Application Deadline and will exercise reasonable
efforts to obtain the New Loan.

5.2. New Loan Terms. If the interest rate, loan costs, monthly payment or
other terms of the New Loan are not satisfactory to Buyer, in Buyer's sole
subjective discretion, Buyer may terminate this Contract by written notice
received by Seller on or before the New Loan Terms Deadline.

6. APPRAISAL PROVISIONS.

6.2. Appraisal Condition. If the Property is not valued at or above the
Purchase Price by an appraiser engaged by Buyer's lender, Buyer may deliver an
Appraisal Objection to Seller on or before the Appraisal Deadline. If the
parties have not reached agreement in writing by the Appraisal Resolution
Deadline, this Contract will terminate.

8. TITLE.

8.1. Evidence of Record Title. Seller will cause a current commitment for an
owner's title insurance policy to be delivered to Buyer on or before the
Record Title Deadline.

8.2. Record Title Objection. Buyer has the right to review the title
commitment and the documents listed in its exception schedule, and to deliver
written objection to Seller on or before the Record Title Objection Deadline.

8.5. Title Resolution. If any title objection is not resolved in writing by
the Title Resolution Deadline, this Contract will terminate and the Earnest
Money will be returned to Buyer.

10. INSPECTION AND DUE DILIGENCE.

10.3. Inspection. Buyer may, at Buyer's expense, have the Property inspected.
Buyer may deliver a written Inspection Objection on or before the Inspection
Objection Deadline, or may terminate this Contract without cause on or before
the Inspection Termination Deadline. If Buyer delivers an Inspection Objection
and the parties have not resolved it in writing by the Inspection Resolution
Deadline, this Contract will terminate.

10.5. Property Insurance. If the cost or availability of property insurance is
not satisfactory to Buyer, Buyer may terminate on or before the Property
Insurance Objection Deadline.

12. CLOSING.

12.3. Closing will take place on the Closing Date at the office of Front Range
Title and Escrow LLC, 2210 Federal Boulevard, Denver, Colorado, or at such
other place as the parties agree in writing.

17. POSSESSION. Possession will be delivered to Buyer on the Possession Date
at the Possession Time, subject to no tenancy or other occupancy.

28. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS.

28.1. Seller will convey the washer and dryer with the Property, at no
additional cost to Buyer, in their present working condition.

28.2. Seller will complete repair of the north basement egress window well
prior to Closing, at Seller's expense, by a licensed contractor, and will
provide the paid invoice at Closing.

28.3. Buyer acknowledges that the Property is served by a domestic well and
that Seller has delivered a copy of the well permit issued by the Colorado
Division of Water Resources.
